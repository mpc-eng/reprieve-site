Privacy Policy

Reprieve is a migraine diary for iPhone, published by MPC Digital Ltd. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: 7 September 2026

The short version. Your migraine record — attacks, symptoms, triggers, medications, doses and anything Reprieve reads from the Health app — is stored on your iPhone and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no cloud sync. We never sell, license or share your health data, including with pharmaceutical companies or researchers.

Nothing at all leaves your device: Reprieve has no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. There is no copy of anything for us — or anyone — to collect.

1. Who we are

Reprieve is developed and published by MPC Digital Ltd, London, United Kingdom. For anything covered by this policy, contact hello@mpcdigital.co.uk.

Because your data never reaches us, we do not hold, process or have any means of accessing it. Reprieve is a free app with no analytics and no in-app purchases, so there is no personal data for which we act as controller beyond the correspondence you choose to send us by email.

2. What Reprieve stores on your device

Everything you enter is written to on-device storage on your iPhone. This includes:

  • Attacks — date and time, duration, the intensity you rated, pain locations, symptoms and any notes you add
  • Triggers and other labels you record against an attack, including labels such as “weather” or “poor sleep” that you enter yourself
  • Medications, doses and how you rated each dose
  • Appointment dates, if you choose to record one
  • App settings, and your answers to the setup questions

This data is special-category health data under UK GDPR (Article 9). Keeping it exclusively on your device, under your control, is the central design decision in Reprieve.

Apple Health (optional)

If you use the optional views that show what else you had logged on your attack days, Reprieve asks permission to read two categories from the Health app: Sleep Analysis and Cycle Tracking. This is used only to show, on your device, when those were recorded alongside your attacks.

  • Reprieve reads from Health. It never writes to Health.
  • Health data is processed on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Permission is requested only when you first open that feature, never during setup.
  • You can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. The rest of the app continues to work unchanged.

What Reprieve does not collect at all

  • No name, email address, password or user account
  • No location data — Reprieve does not use Location Services and has no weather service; “weather” is only ever a label you type
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), no ad networks and no cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • No web cookies — and this website sets none, loads no fonts or scripts from third parties, and runs no analytics

3. What leaves your device: nothing

Reprieve collects no data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier and no in-app purchases. It sends no usage events, no crash reports and no diagnostics to us or to any third party. There is no Reprieve server for anything to be sent to.

This is enforced in the app's source code, not merely by policy: the app links no analytics or advertising SDK, and no payment or subscription service, of any kind.

The only company involved at all is Apple, and only in its normal role as the App Store: Apple knows you downloaded a free app, in the same way it does for any app. Apple never receives your migraine record, which stays on your device. Apple's own privacy policy governs that relationship.

We do not sell, rent, license or otherwise make available any of your data to advertisers, data brokers, pharmaceutical companies, insurers, employers or researchers — and because we never receive it, we could not do so even if we wanted to. We do not use health or fitness data for advertising, marketing or data-mining purposes of any kind.

4. Notifications

Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally on your device. Reprieve has no push server, so no notification content is ever transmitted. If you allow notifications, you may receive an occasional weekly summary or a reminder before an appointment you recorded. You can turn any of these off in the app or in iOS Settings.

5. Exports and backups

The doctor-report PDF and the encrypted backup file are generated on your device, at your request. Reprieve then hands the file to the standard iOS share sheet, and it goes wherever you send it — AirDrop, Files, Mail, a printer, or an app of your choosing. Reprieve does not upload these files anywhere, and does not keep a copy beyond the one on your device.

Once you share a file, its privacy depends on where you sent it. No personal health information is stored in iCloud by Reprieve.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your record is kept on your device for as long as you want it. There is no expiry and no archiving.
  • You can delete individual attacks and doses at any time.
  • You can erase everything Reprieve holds in one action from the app's settings.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device data with it.
  • Because your record was never copied off the device, deleting it really is the end of it — there is no backup of it on our side to reach.

7. Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

  • Your health record. Processed entirely on your own device, under your control. We are not a recipient of it and have no ability to access it.
  • Apple Health access. Your explicit consent, given through the iOS permission prompt and revocable at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Email you send us. Where you contact support, we process your message on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. We ask you not to include health details.

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. In Reprieve these rights are exercised directly in the app:

  • Access — your full record is visible in the app at any time
  • Rectification — edit or correct any entry
  • Erasure — delete individual entries, or erase everything from settings
  • Portability — export your record as a PDF or an encrypted backup file you control
  • Withdraw consent — revoke Health or notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first at hello@mpcdigital.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk.

9. International transfers

Your record does not leave your device, so there is no international transfer of your data. Reprieve uses no overseas analytics, hosting or payment providers.

10. Children

Reprieve is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from them.

11. Medical disclaimer

Reprieve is a personal record-keeping app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, prediction or treatment. Trends, counts and per-dose summaries are descriptive read-backs of what you entered, and nothing more. Always speak to a qualified healthcare professional about your symptoms, your medication and any decision about your care.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. If a change materially affects what leaves your device, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
