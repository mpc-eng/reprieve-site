What Reprieve is

A record, not an oracle

Reprieve only ever shows you what you put in. Nothing is inferred and nothing is added. That is what makes it your record, rather than somebody’s guess about you.

  • Records your attacks, symptoms, triggers, medications and doses, exactly as you enter them.
  • Shows your own history back to you, as counts, averages and plain summaries.
  • Hands it over as a PDF, so you and your doctor can look at the same thing.
What Reprieve is not

Things Reprieve will never do

Reprieve is not a medical device, and it never guesses about your health. It shows you what you wrote down and leaves the reading of it to you and your doctor.

  • No forecasts. Reprieve will never tell you an attack is coming or score your risk.
  • No diagnosis. It counts what you logged. It does not name a condition or pass a verdict.
  • No dosing advice. No calculator, no “best medication”, no suggestion to change what you take.
  • No data sale. Your record is not aggregated, licensed or sold to anyone, including pharmaceutical companies.

Questions

Before you switch

Is the trend graph really free?

Yes. Your intensity over time, how you rated each dose, and the PDF report are the free app. You do not have to pay to see any of it, and you will not be asked to.

Can Reprieve tell me when a migraine is coming?

No, and it never will. Reprieve shows you what you have already recorded — counts, averages and plain summaries. It does not forecast attacks, score your risk or interpret your data on your behalf.

That is on purpose. An app that predicted attacks or named a condition would be making a medical judgement about you, and that is your doctor's job, not your diary's.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your iPhone. There is no Reprieve server, no account and no cloud sync, so there is no copy of your record for anyone — including us — to read, lose or sell.

The only thing that leaves your device is anonymous usage counts, which contain no health information. The privacy policy lists exactly what that covers.

Can I move my logs across from another app?

Not yet. Reprieve starts clean, and your trend line begins with the first attack you log. Bringing old logs across is something we are looking at — if it matters to you, tell us, because it helps us know how much it matters.

Does Reprieve use Apple Health?

Only if you turn it on, and only for the optional views that show what else you had logged on your attack days. With your permission Reprieve reads Sleep and Cycle Tracking so it can show when those were recorded alongside your attacks. It reads on your device, writes nothing back, and uploads nothing. You can withdraw the permission in iOS Settings at any time, and the rest of the app carries on working exactly as before.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 17.4 or later. Reprieve works fully with no permissions granted at all — notifications and Health access are both optional, and neither is requested before you have logged anything.

Is Reprieve available yet?

Not on the App Store. The app is built and being tested ahead of submission, and this page will link to it the day it is live. If you would like to try the beta or hear when it ships, get in touch.

Reprieve is a personal record-keeping app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Everything it displays is a summary of what you entered. Always speak to a qualified healthcare professional about your symptoms, your medication and any decision about your care.
